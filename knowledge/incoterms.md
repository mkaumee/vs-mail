# Incoterms: who pays what

The Incoterm on the booking decides which party carries each cost. It is the
answer to most "should I be billed for this?" questions.

| Term | Seller pays | Buyer pays |
|---|---|---|
| **EXW** — ex works | Nothing beyond making goods available | Everything: origin haulage, origin THC, freight, destination THC, duty |
| **FOB** — free on board | Origin haulage, export clearance, origin THC | Ocean freight, destination THC, duty |
| **CFR** — cost and freight | Origin THC, **ocean freight** | Destination THC, duty, insurance |
| **CIF** — cost, insurance, freight | Origin THC, ocean freight, **insurance** | Destination THC, duty |

## The two that cause most queries

**CFR.** The seller has paid the freight, so the buyer often expects nothing
further — then receives a destination THC invoice. That is correct. CFR ends
at the discharge port; everything from the ship onward is the buyer's.

**FOB.** Risk passes at the load port, so origin THC is the seller's and
everything after is the buyer's — including ocean freight, which buyers on
FOB terms sometimes expect to have been included.

## Where it is recorded

The Incoterm is on the booking and is repeated on the invoice. Where a query
is about who should carry a charge, the Incoterm on that specific booking
settles it — not the general pattern above, which is only what is usual.
