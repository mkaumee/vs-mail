# Reference material

What the answering side of the system retrieves from.

## ⚠️ None of this came from the organizers

The bundle ships 520 emails and 250 SI/BL attachments. It ships no tariff
sheet, no charge policy and no invoice records — and the invoice queries carry
no attachments at all. Everything in this folder was written for this project.

The policy documents describe ordinary liner-shipping practice, written to be
accurate. **`records.json` is fabricated.** The invoice numbers and booking
references in it are the real ones from the bundle's emails, so a question
about invoice 5250075931 finds a record — but every amount, date and status
attached to them was generated.

Any answer drawn from a record says so on screen and in the drafted reply. A
system that quotes an invented charge to a customer without marking it is
worse than one that declines to answer, so the marking is not decoration.

## What is here

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| `charges.md` | THC, local charges, what ocean freight includes |
| `detention.md` | Detention and demurrage, free time, disputes |
| `billing.md` | Goods receipt, PGI, why billing blocks, cancellations |
| `documents.md` | The BL lifecycle, drafts, cut-offs, telex release |
| `incoterms.md` | Who pays what under CFR, FOB, CIF, EXW |
| `records.json` | **Fabricated** invoice, charge and GR records |

Chunked and embedded by `scripts/build_index.py`.
